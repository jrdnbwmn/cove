# Strategy Brief

This document outlines key pieces of strategy that are useful to know while building.

For product rules (families, students, billing behavior), see
[product-brief.md](product-brief.md).

## Main idea

The core idea is a chief of staff for homeschool families that reduces the planning, administrative work, and anxiety involved in homeschooling. It would proactively help parents:

- Build plans and stay on track
- Know what to do each day
- Organize learning and measure progress
- Manage portfolios, state requirements, and college preparation
- Adapt to different homeschooling approaches
- Automate as much administrative work as possible

The assistant should remain gentle, nonjudgmental, and parent-controlled. Like a real chief of staff, it prepares options, recommends a path, handles logistics, and follows up—but leaves decisions to the parent.

After using our product, running a homeschool should feel much lighter and much more effective.

## Positioning & differentiation

We differentiate ourselves from other homeschooling tools by:

Most families today run their homeschool on spreadsheets, paper planners,
and general-purpose apps. We differentiate by:

- **Design quality is the product.** Every screen should feel beautiful, calm, clear, and obvious to a stressed parent.
- **AI prepares, the parent decides.** AI drafts plans, suggests next steps,
  and handles paperwork, but never acts on its own. Every AI feature has a
  non-AI path, so a parent who never touches AI still gets a complete
  product.
- **Works across homeschooling approaches.** We support traditional,
  Charlotte Mason, classical, unschooling, eclectic, etc. The product adapts
  to the family's approach instead of assuming a specific model.
- **Built from real homeschool problems.** Features start from a specific
  problem parents actually have (e.g. "what do I do today?", "am I meeting
  my state's requirements?"), not from what other tools include.

## Target market

US homeschooling families, K–12, at any level of homeschooling experience.

- **We design for the parent who buys.** They're the one planning and running the homeschool.
- **Families only, not co-ops, micro-schools, or other schools.** The Premium student cap exists to enforce this.

## Business model

Two tiers on the family account: **Free** (no subscription) and **Premium**. Flat price per family, not per student. For current prices, see the pricing page (`/pricing`).

- **Free** covers limited use for small families.
- **Premium** adds automation, AI, and other useful features.

**No trial.** Free serves that purpose.
